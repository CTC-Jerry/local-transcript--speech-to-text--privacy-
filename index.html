How it works

Four steps, no setup

1

Open the side panel

Click the Local Transcript icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the panel on any tab.

2

Choose a save file

Pick a text file on your computer. The transcript will be written there in real time.

3

Press Start

The extension begins transcribing the tab's audio immediately. No waiting, no loading bar.

4

Press Stop — or walk away

Recording stops when you press Stop, when the video ends, or after a period of silence.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Local Transcript (Speech-to-Text) is a Chrome extension that transcribes audio from browser tabs using on-device speech recognition. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected.

Data we do not collect

Local Transcript does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Specifically:

  • Audio captured from your browser tabs is processed locally and immediately discarded. It is never recorded to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Transcripts are written directly to a file you choose on your own computer. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • No analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry are sent.
  • No account or login is required. The extension has no back-end service.

Permissions used

  • tabCapture — captures the audio output of the tab you choose to transcribe. Audio is processed on-device and never stored or transmitted.
  • offscreen — runs the speech recognition model in a background document so transcription continues without blocking the browser UI.
  • sidePanel — displays the live transcript in the Chrome side panel.
  • storage — saves your preferences (e.g. whether timestamps are enabled) locally in Chrome. No personal data is stored here.
  • activeTab — scopes audio capture to the specific tab you have opened the panel on.
  • scripting — injects a small listener to detect when media playback ends, so recording can stop automatically.
  • alarms — periodically checks whether the AI model should be unloaded from memory after a period of inactivity.

Third-party services

The extension uses the open-source Whisper model (whisper-base.en) by OpenAI, bundled inside the extension package. The model runs entirely on your device. No data is sent to OpenAI or any other third party.
