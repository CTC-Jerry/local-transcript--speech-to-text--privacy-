Local Transcript — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Local Transcript processes all audio entirely on your own computer. No audio, transcript text, or any other personal data is ever sent to any server.

What Local Transcript does

Local Transcript is a Chrome extension that transcribes the audio playing in a browser tab using a speech recognition model that runs locally on your device. The transcript is saved to a text file you choose on your own computer.

Data we collect

Local Transcript does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • Audio: The audio from the tab you choose to transcribe is captured, processed by the on-device speech model, and then discarded. It is never stored permanently and never transmitted to any server or third party.
  • Transcript text: The transcript is written to a text file on your computer at the location you choose. It is never sent anywhere outside your device.
  • Preferences: Your settings (such as whether timestamps are enabled) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. They never leave your device.
  • Usage data: No usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind are collected.

Permissions used and why

  • tabCapture: To capture the audio playing in the browser tab you want to transcribe. Audio is processed locally and immediately discarded after transcription.
  • offscreen: To run the speech recognition model in a background page so it can work while you use other parts of the browser normally.
  • sidePanel: To display the live transcript in a panel alongside the page you are watching.
  • storage: To remember your preferences (such as the timestamps setting) between browser sessions. No personal data is stored.
  • activeTab: To obtain permission to capture audio from the currently active tab when you press Start. This permission is granted only for the tab you explicitly activate it on.
  • scripting: To inject a small script into the tab being captured so the extension can detect when media playback ends and automatically stop the recording.
  • alarms: To free up the memory used by the speech recognition model (several hundred MB) after a period of inactivity, and reload it when needed again.

Third-party services

Local Transcript does not communicate with any external service. The speech recognition model (OpenAI Whisper, whisper-base.en) is bundled inside the extension and runs entirely on your device using your browser's built-in WebAssembly and WebGPU capabilities.

If you have enabled the optional ad slot, an advertisement may be loaded from a third-party ad provider. The ad runs in a sandboxed iframe with no access to the extension, the tab being captured, or any transcript. Please refer to the ad provider's own privacy policy for information about how they handle data.

Children's privacy

Local Transcript does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
